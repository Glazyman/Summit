The part nobody warns you about

You only get to sell your business once. The buyer has done this a hundred times.

That's the trap. You're the best in the world at running your company, but the people across the table buy companies every week. They know every lever, every clause and every delay tactic. Most owners lose money on their exit in one of three ways:

Mistake #1

Waiting until you're burned out

Buyers pay for the trend, not the history. When an owner is tired, it shows up in the numbers: flat growth, deferred hiring, slipping margins. A business sold from strength is worth more than the same business sold from exhaustion.

Mistake #2

Taking the first buyer who calls

The buyer who reaches out first is rarely the buyer who pays most, or the one who keeps your team. With only one bidder, you have no leverage, and they know it.

Mistake #3

Looking at the price, not the deal

Asset sale or stock sale can mean $500K to $1M or more in taxes on a $10M deal. Earnouts can inflate the headline number while shrinking what you actually take home. The number on the letter isn't the number in your bank account.
